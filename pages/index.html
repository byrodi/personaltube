PersonalTube GitHub

Your own YouTube home feed, picked by AI from your watch history.

One page, endless scroll. It runs on your computer, learns from what you open, like and hide, and finds creators across languages.

Install Read the README

Install

You need Python 3.10 or newer and pipx or uv.

pipx install git+https://github.com/byrodi/personaltube
uv tool install git+https://github.com/byrodi/personaltube
git clone https://github.com/byrodi/personaltube
cd personaltube
pipx install .

Start it

personaltube

Your browser opens http://localhost:8777 with a setup page of three steps:

  1. Pick the AI and press "Check and save".
  2. Pick the browser you are signed in to YouTube with, or upload a Google Takeout file.
  3. Press "Build my profile", then "Open my feed".

The first videos show up within a minute. More keep coming while the first search runs, for about 20 minutes.

How it works

  1. It reads your YouTube history, likes and subscriptions, read-only.
  2. An AI model turns them into 12 to 20 interests with search queries and channels.
  3. It searches YouTube anonymously and checks new uploads of good channels.
  4. The AI rates every video from 0 to 10. Only 6 and higher reach your feed.
  5. The feed ranks by score, freshness and your clicks, and refreshes once a day.

Privacy

  • Everything stays in ~/.personaltube on your computer.
  • The site listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
  • Searching for new videos is anonymous.
  • Your AI provider sees video titles and channel names.
  • With a local model, nothing leaves your computer.
